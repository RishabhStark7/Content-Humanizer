Medicines are meant to improve your health, but only when they are stored correctly, handled carefully, and used within their shelf life.

At Tata 1mg, date-lapsed product, rejected batch, rejected, or recalled healthcare orders are never sold to customers. They are identified through defined quality processes, removed from active inventory, and handled through authorized return or disposal processes.

This page explains how these healthcare orders are identified, why they are removed, and what happens to them after they leave our active inventory.

Every healthcare order has a defined shelf life.

The expiry date tells you how long the manufacturer expects the healthcare order to maintain its quality when stored according to the recommended conditions.

Using an date-lapsed product healthcare order may mean that it no longer performs as intended.

Similarly, healthcare orders with rejected batch packaging, broken seals, or signs of tampering may no longer be suitable for use.

Medicine safety begins long before an order is placed.

Packaging condition

This traceability helps us know where every healthcare order came from, where it is stored, and whether it remains eligible for dispatch.
