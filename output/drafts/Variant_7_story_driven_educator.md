Every prescribed care item has a defined shelf life.

The expiry date tells you how long the manufacturer expects the prescribed care item to maintain its quality when stored according to the recommended conditions.

Using an lapsed shelf-life prescribed care item may mean that it no longer performs as intended.

Similarly, prescribed care items with unusable item packaging, broken seals, or signs of tampering may no longer be suitable for use.

This is why identifying these products before they reach patients is an important part of prescribed care item safety.

Medicine safety begins long before an order is placed.

When prescribed care items arrive at a Tata 1mg fulfilment centre, they go through an inward verification process before they become available for customer orders.

Manufacturer information

Packaging condition

This traceability helps us know where every prescribed care item came from, where it is stored, and whether it remains eligible for dispatch.
