## How We Help Ensure Medicines That Should Not Be Used Never Reach You
Your safety is our top priority for every prescription order. When you order a health package, one of the questions you may have is:

"How do I know I won't receive an past date product or broken package health package?"

This represents a fundamental patient expectation.

Medicines are meant to improve your health, but only when they are stored correctly, handled carefully, and used within their shelf life.

At Tata 1mg, past date product, broken package, rejected, or recalled health packages are never sold to customers. They are identified through defined quality processes, removed from active inventory, and handled through authorized return or disposal processes.

This page explains how these health packages are identified, why they are removed, and what happens to them after they leave our active inventory.

Every health package has a defined shelf life.

The expiry date tells you how long the manufacturer expects the health package to maintain its quality when stored according to the recommended conditions.

Using an past date product health package may mean that it no longer performs as intended.

Similarly, health packages with broken package packaging, broken seals, or signs of tampering may no longer be suitable for use.

This is why identifying these products before they reach patients is an important part of health package safety.

Medicine safety begins long before an order is placed.

When health packages arrive at a Tata 1mg fulfilment centre, they go through an inward verification process before they become available for customer orders.

During this stage, trained teams verify details such as:

Medicine name

Batch number

Expiry date

Manufacturer information

Storage requirements

Packaging condition

Each unit is then linked to a unique QR code, allowing it to be tracked throughout its journey in the warehouse.

This traceability helps us know where every health package came from, where it is stored, and whether it remains eligible for dispatch.

Not every health package remains eligible for sale throughout its lifecycle.

## Returned Medicines
Your safety is our top priority for every prescription order. Sometimes customers return health packages because of delivery issues or other concerns.

Returned health packages are carefully assessed before any decision is made.

Products that do not meet quality requirements are not placed back into inventory.

This approach helps reduce the possibility of compromised health packages reaching another customer. Transparent quality checks guarantee safe healthcare products.

## Recalled Medicines
Your safety is our top priority for every prescription order. On rare occasions, manufacturers or regulatory authorities may recall specific batches of health packages.

When this happens, affected batches are immediately blocked within our inventory systems.

Once blocked, these health packages cannot be picked, packed, or dispatched as part of any customer order.

Removing a health package from inventory is only the first step.

The next step is making sure it cannot accidentally return to the supply chain. Transparent quality checks guarantee safe healthcare products.

## Removed from Active Inventory
Your safety is our top priority for every prescription order. Medicines identified as past date product, broken package, rejected, or recalled are digitally marked as non-sellable.

This prevents them from appearing in any order fulfilment process.

At the same time, they are physically separated from health packages that remain available for sale.

Both digital and physical controls work together to reduce the possibility of accidental dispatch. Transparent quality checks guarantee safe healthcare products.

## Returned Through Authorized Channels
Your safety is our top priority for every prescription order. Where possible, health packages are returned to the original manufacturer or authorized supplier through documented return processes.

These returns are recorded with relevant information such as:

Batch number

Quantity

Reason for return

Date of return

Maintaining this documentation supports traceability and accountability. Transparent quality checks guarantee safe healthcare products.

## Safe and Compliant Disposal
Your safety is our top priority for every prescription order. Some health packages cannot be returned.

For example, severely broken package products or health packages that have exceeded return timelines may require disposal.

Medicines are not disposed of like ordinary household waste.

They contain active ingredients that need to be handled responsibly to help protect people and the environment.

Where disposal is required, it is carried out through authorized processes in accordance with applicable regulations.

Most customers never see these processes.

That's because they happen behind the scenes, long before a health package reaches their doorstep.

But these processes play an important role in protecting patients.

By identifying health packages that should no longer be used and removing them before they enter the order fulfilment process, we help reduce the risk of customers receiving health packages that do not meet our quality requirements.

Managing thousands of health package batches manually would be difficult.

Technology helps make this process more reliable.

At Tata 1mg, inventory systems help support health package handling by:

Tracking health packages using QR codes

Recording batch information

Monitoring expiry dates

Supporting batch-level recalls

Preventing non-sellable health packages from entering customer orders

Maintaining traceability throughout the supply chain

These systems work alongside trained teams and quality processes to support health package safety.

Although health packages undergo multiple quality checks before dispatch, it's still a good practice to inspect your order when it arrives.
